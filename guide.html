What is Naked Brain

Naked Brain is the phone app of the FarmBox. It has two parts, the two buttons at the top: Tasks is your work of the day, with its checklists and the scouting. Capture is for notes: say it, photograph it or film it, and send it to the right list in Notion. What you do in Tasks shows in Naked Heart, the console on the computer.

Your tasks

The work planned for you, day by day. Tap a task to do its checklist.

A timer on every task

It starts when you open the checklist. Nothing to fill in.

Scouting

Trap cards and plant photos, zone by zone. The phone counts and measures.

Voice notes

Say it. The app writes it down and can sum it up.

Photos and video

In a checklist, in the scouting and in a note.

Works without signal

What you do is kept on the phone and goes up when there is signal.

Install it on your phone

Open https://brain.nkd.farm/ in the browser of your phone and add it to the home screen. It then opens like any other app.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

Tap the Share button (box with arrow) → Add to Home Screen → Add

Android (Chrome)

Tap the ⋮ menu → Add to Home Screen → Install

Computer (Chrome / Edge)

Click the install icon (⊕) in the address bar → Install

The first screen of the app

You sign in with your own FarmBox account: the same e-mail and password as in Naked Heart. An Admin makes your account on the Team page of Naked Heart and gives you the e-mail and the password.

  • Type your e-mail and your password and tap Sign in. The button says Signing in…, then Reading your tasks….
  • Signing in needs signal. After that the app works without it.
  • One sign-in is for the whole app: Tasks and Capture.
  • Your name comes from your account. You do not type it.
  • You stay signed in. If the app says Please sign in again, sign in again: your work is kept on the phone.
No team password. The app no longer asks one. Only if you open Capture on a phone that is not signed in can a screen ask for a "team password": tap Back to Tasks, sign in, and open Capture again. If it still asks, tell the Admin.

The day list (an example): one card a task

The app opens on Tasks: the work planned in Naked Heart, day by day.

  • Whose tasks. The menu above the list opens on your name. Choose Everyone, Nobody yet or another person to see their tasks. With two units a second menu shows both units or one.
  • Today is open, with Morning and Afternoon. Its heading counts: how many are done, the time worked, what is left.
  • Earlier holds the last seven days, folded. A red dot means something was left undone: tap to finish it.
  • The days ahead are folded too. Tap a day to open it.
  • Closed today folds the tasks already done or cancelled today.
  • A task with its timer running shows at the top as In progress, with Pause and Open.

What a card says.

Title and place
The task, then where: the zone, or the zones to visit
What it asks
"5 inputs" or "photos", then the planned minutes. A card that asks nothing can be swiped done
Who
The people responsible, with their faces — or "nobody yet"
Its state, on the right
In progress, Paused, Done with who and how long, Cancelled with why, Not done for a day gone by
A lock and a name
Somebody else has this task open. It opens for you when they finish or hand it over
Colours. The colour of a card is its family: green for Agriculture, blue for Maintenance, amber for Office.
Doing a task: the checklist
1

Tap the task

Its checklist opens and its timer starts. A task with no checklist only asks: mark it done? A task of a later day asks before it starts early.

2

Read what it needs

Under the title: where to go, about how long, the protection to wear (PPE) and the tools. Show all details opens the full instruction of every step; Details opens one step.

3

Answer each step

A step is a button, red until you tap it green (✓ OK or ✓ Done). A step that asks a number has a box: type the reading, the allowed range is beside it. A step that asks a photo has a Photo button. A check over the zones shows one button a zone, and All OK.

4

Say what is not OK

Under the steps there is one box, Notes, and Add a photo. If something is not OK, write what: the manager is told. A photo has a small ✕ in its corner to remove it.

5

Press Finish

Finish sends the report and goes back to the list. If a step has no answer it says how many are left. A step still red is asked about once and sent as not OK, with your note.

The timer. It shows in the top bar. Tap it to pause, tap it again to go on. The back arrow pauses it too and keeps your answers. Left alone, it stops about 20 minutes after your last touch.
Good to know. If the phone closes the app, the checklist comes back as you left it, photos included. Under Finish: Hand over to somebody else… sends what you entered so far and frees the task for the next person; Cancel this task… is for a task that will not be done. A done task opens again to add photos and notes or to correct it: Save the changes. In a harvest task, type the kilograms cut from each position and tick Last cut when the position is cleared.
Swipe: done, cancel, Undo

On the day list a card can be swiped, without opening it.

1

Swipe right: done

For a task that asks nothing. If the task needs readings or photos the app says what it needs and offers Open.

2

Swipe left: cancel

Choose why — Not needed, Equipment not available, Weather or access, Other — and write why in the note: the manager reads it. Then Cancel the task, or Keep it. Only the people on the task, or the Admin, can cancel it.

3

Undo

After a swipe a bar shows for five seconds with Undo. Tap it to take the swipe back.

The daily scouting

The scouting of the day is one card. Tap it: it lists its zones, each a task of its own with its own timer, and one card on top, All · whole greenhouse.

1

Open a zone

The screen has tabs: Traps, Health, Growth and Review. A red dot on a tab means it is required today and not complete yet; green means it is, or that it is not asked today. A tab that says "not today" still opens: more is always welcome.

2

Traps

Photograph each sticky card square-on, the whole card in the picture: one side, then the other. The phone reads the position written on the card (1B6 = zone 1, row B, 6 m) and counts the insects. Confirm the position and correct the count if it is wrong. A trap taken down or out of reach is marked in its row. No traps today skips the traps of the zone.

3

Health

A photo of anything that looks wrong on a plant, close and in focus. Add where it is if you want (row and metres, B6) and a comment. ✦ AI note asks the AI about one photo, only when you press it; it needs signal.

4

Growth

A photo of a fruit, a leaf or a head with the printed scale card flat beside it, all of the card in the picture. The phone measures against the card. A growth photo without the card is not kept: take it again.

5

Say if a section is not OK

Each tab ends with a green line: the section is OK. Something wrong? Tap it, it turns red, and write the problem under it. The manager is told.

6

Review, then Finish

Review shows everything you captured. Finish sends the zone and goes back to the list of zones. If a section is under its minimum the app asks once before it sends.

All · whole greenhouse. Not a task and never required. Open it to say or write what you saw across the greenhouse today: Record a voice note (up to 3 minutes) or write it, then Send the note. It goes in the daily report in Naked Heart, above the zones.
The full rules are in the app: in a zone, open How the scouting works under the title. It says which section is due on which day, the minimum of each, how to write the position on a card and how the sizes are measured.
Extra scouting and feedback

Extra scouting

  • The button ＋ Extra scouting is above the day list. Anybody can use it, at any time.
  • Pick a zone and add photos and counts. They go into today's report, marked as extra.
  • There is no minimum. Send a zone as often as you like, all day.
  • Going back from a zone sends what you captured there.
  • The planned scouting of each zone stays its own task.
  • When you are done for the day, swipe the Extra scouting card: right for done, left for cancelled. The person who started it does this, or the Admin.

Feedback on a task

  • When the farm has switched it on, every task card has a small speech-bubble button, and the checklist has Feedback beside its title.
  • Use it to say what should change in the task, its checklist or the app.
  • Choose the step it is about, or the whole task.
  • Record a voice note (up to 3 minutes), write, add photos or a video under a minute.
  • Send the feedback. Without signal it is kept and goes later.
  • Under the form you see what was already said on the task, and the answer.
Sending, sync and no signal

You never lose work for lack of signal. Everything you do is kept on the phone first and goes up by itself when there is signal.

Offline
A small sign in the Tasks bar when the phone has no connection. Carry on working
waiting to send
On a done card: the report is on the phone and has not reached the server yet
The line under the list
When it last synced, how many reports wait, how many photos are still uploading. "Could not be sent — tap to try again": tap it
⟳ Sync now
Top right of Tasks. It sends what waits and reads the plan again. The app also does it by itself, about every minute while it is open
Force send is the button beside ⟳. The normal way to send is Finish inside a task. Force send is for clearing the phone: it sends everything the phone still holds, as it stands, even if it is not complete. It explains itself and asks before it runs, then lists what went and what could not go; what cannot be sent at all can be removed from the phone there. It needs signal. Close an open checklist first. Harvest weights are never forced: finish them in their task.
Photos. The report goes first; the full photos follow. In Settings you choose whether they upload on Wi-Fi only or also on mobile data.
Before you give the phone to somebody else, sign out (Settings › Account). Sign out first sends what is left. If something still could not go, it tells you and asks: signed out, it would go up later under the next person's name.

The AI button writes the summary · the tab's own fields · Send

The four tabs. Each sends to its own list in Notion. The big title on the screen says what the tab is for.

Ugly200 — Log an issue
Something wrong or worth noting. Fields: Tags, Status (Open, In Progress, Done), Location
SoP — Capture a procedure
Talk or film the steps of a way of working. Fields: Category, Status
Daily — Daily update
What got done, and what needs deciding. The Title is your name. Fields: Date, FarmBox1, FarmLab, Tech Plan, Decisions needed. No Note box
SU reports — SU report
Ideas to improve FarmBox, from the Stellenbosch University team. Choose the Category (Agronomy, Tech, General) and write your own Title: both are required. Then Date and, if it helps, Zone
Which one? What you saw in the crops today goes in the scouting, under All — not in SU reports.
1

Choose the tab

Tap Capture at the top, then the tab. The Send button says where the note goes.

2

Give it a title

A few words. On Ugly200 and SoP you may leave it empty: the summary or the first words are used. On SU reports it is required.

3

Say it

Tap Voice to record, tap again to stop. The words appear by themselves in Voice transcription. Record again and the new words are added. You can also type in any box.

4

Add photos or a video

Photo adds pictures. Video adds a video of 8 minutes at most: the app makes it smaller and writes what is said in Video transcription. Tap the ✕ on a picture to remove it. Add location adds your GPS position: wait for the precision you want, then Save location.

5

Sum it up, if you want

Tap AI beside Voice summary or Video summary: the app writes a few short points. Copy copies a box; Clear empties it and asks first.

6

Fill the tab's fields

Tap the chips to choose them; + Add makes a new one. On Daily and SU reports the date is today unless you change it.

7

Send

Send to … at the bottom makes the page in Notion with everything on the form. It needs signal. Without it the app says nothing was sent and your note stays: press Send again later.

Drafts. What you type or dictate is kept on the phone as you go. If the app is closed it comes back and says Draft restored. Photos and videos are not kept: attach them again.
No signal while recording? The recording stays on the note as Voice note — tap to transcribe. Tap it when there is signal, or send the note as it is.
Recent entries, at the bottom of Capture, lists the last notes sent from this phone, each with ✓ Sent to Notion or ✗ Failed.
Settings

Tap the gear at the top right. The back arrow, or the Back of the phone, closes it and leaves your task as it was.

App updates

The app looks for a new version every 2 minutes, and each time you come back to it.

By itself
When nothing is open — no checklist, no camera, no typing, no recording, nothing attached to a note — it updates alone: "Updating to …", then "Updated to v …"
While you work
It waits. A card shows at the top instead, and nothing waiting to be sent is lost
A new version is available
A new version of Naked Brain is ready
Update now
✕

Tap Update now to update at once. Tap ✕ to put the card away: the app then updates by itself when nothing is open. If the button says Not here yet, the new version has not reached your connection: it is offered again in a few minutes. If the card says it could not finish updating, close Naked Brain completely and open it again.

Which version do I have? Settings › App shows it, and Settings › Version log says what each version changed.
